## Offshore Structured Products

- Provides exposure to an index of foreign shares.

 Investment period options between 3 to 5 years.

 Guaranteed returns based on performance of index. Typically, between 15% - 20% per annum for Rand
denominated products and between 8% - 12% per annum for foreign currency denominated products.

 100% capital protection up -40% growth in the index.

 You can choose between foreign currency (USD, GBP or AUD) or Rands.

 Pure capital growth investment, no tax implications given that Namibia does not have capital gains tax
yet.

 Minimum requirement: N$100,000 to N$300,000 (depending on product’s terms and conditions).

 Products comes up Ad Hoc and on availability – Currently on offer Britannic Opportunities Ltd.

 Investec allows exit through secondary trades (if sold before Term ends).

quarterly, or annual period, rather than at its conclusion.
